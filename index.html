ABOUT US

Our Journey

Started in 2016 with 2 broadlooms, and have grown to 10 broadlooms to date. In 2018, we set up hand-tufting, and by 2021 we added our pitloom setup. Today, we run a complete in-house workshop, from start to finish.

  2. 2016

    Started with 2 broadlooms.

  3. 2018

    Hand-tufted set-up added.

  4. 2021

    Pitloom set-up added.

  5. Today

    Complete in-house set-up.
